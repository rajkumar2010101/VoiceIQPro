🧠 How AI Learning Works in VoiceIQ Pro

1
AI Analyzes Your Data
When you upload a CSV or receive data via API, VoiceIQ's NLP engine automatically predicts sentiment, emotion, topic, and escalation risk for every conversation.
2
You Review & Correct Mistakes
Below, you see recent conversations with the AI's predictions. If the AI got something wrong (e.g., said "positive" but it was actually "frustrated"), you correct it using the dropdowns and click Submit Correction.
3
Corrections Are Saved to Database
Your corrections are stored in the SQLite database (voiceiq.db). Each correction is a "lesson" for the AI — what it predicted vs. what was correct.
4
Owner Triggers Retraining
Once enough corrections are collected (5+), the Owner can click "Apply Learning" to update the model. Accuracy improves with every batch of corrections.
